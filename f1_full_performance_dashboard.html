Selected findings from the 20 SQL questions

Season calendars 2024, 2025 had the most races (24 each); 2020 had the fewest (17).
Driver points leader Lewis Hamilton recorded 3,469.5 points and 72 wins in the selected period.
Constructor points leader Mercedes recorded 6,433.5 points, 247 podiums, and a 53.00% podium rate by recorded result rows.
Qualifying vs race Position improvement counts compare final position to qualifying position; they do not directly measure overall driver quality.
Race-to-race changes LAG() compares a driver's finishing position with their previous recorded race; positive change means the finishing-position number improved.
Points averages Driver average-points metrics use recorded result rows as the denominator; interpret them alongside participation counts.
Consistency by season Constructor season rankings compare points earned by each constructor within each year, not cumulative career totals.
